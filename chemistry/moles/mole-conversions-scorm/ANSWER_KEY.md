# Mole Conversions: answer key

Generated by `node tools/answer-key.js` from the model in `src/js/`. Do not edit by hand. SCHEMA 1.

Every student gets their own substances and numbers (a seed saved with their progress), so this key gives the plan, the
rules, every substance and a worked sample set. Answers are computed by the activity from the model and checked by
`tools/validate.js` against a direct mole calculation and the formative quiz worked by hand.

## The class’s rules in the activity

- **Molar masses to the tenths place.** The on-screen periodic table shows every atomic mass rounded to tenths; the
  molar mass is those added up (CCl₄ = 12.0 + 4(35.5) = 154.0). Also accepted: the full masses added and rounded at the
  end (153.8), and the tenths value of a two-decimal table (Fe 55.85 gives 55.9, Cu 63.55 gives 63.6). The answer is
  then checked against the molar mass the student typed.
- **The roads:** ___ g = 1 mol (molar mass, a blank), 6.022 × 10²³ particles = 1 mol, 22.4 L = 1 mol (gases at STP only),
  1 mol compound = ___ mol element (the ratio from the formula, a blank).
- **Particles:** atoms of an element, molecules of a diatomic element or molecular compound. Ionic compounds are never
  counted in particles (their elements are: atoms O in Fe₂O₃).
- **Significant figures:** every equality counts as exact; the given quantity decides. A right number with the wrong
  significant figures is not marked correct; the student is told the rule and tries again.
- **Scoring:** a graded problem counts when it is right before Show solution (Show solution asks to confirm). Hints and
  checking again are free. Score = problems right / 12, to `cmi.core.score.raw`; retakes keep the best.
