## The six types

| Type | General form | What gives it away | Example in the lesson |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Synthesis | A + B → AB | There is only one product. | 2 Mg + O₂ → 2 MgO |
| Decomposition | AB → A + B | There is only one reactant. | 2 H₂O → 2 H₂ + O₂ |
| Single replacement | A + BC → AC + B | An element and a compound make a different element and a different compound. | Zn + 2 HCl → ZnCl₂ + H₂ |
| Double replacement | AB + CD → AD + CB | Two compounds trade partners. | Pb(NO₃)₂ + 2 KI → PbI₂ + 2 KNO₃ |
| Combustion | CxHy + O₂ → CO₂ + H₂O | A compound of carbon and hydrogen reacts with O₂ and makes CO₂ and H₂O. | CH₄ + 2 O₂ → CO₂ + 2 H₂O |
| Neutralization | HX + MOH → MX + HOH | An acid and a base make a salt and water (HOH). | Ca(OH)₂ + 2 HCl → CaCl₂ + 2 HOH |

**Three rules for close calls**

1. One product: synthesis. One reactant: decomposition. Count the substances first.
2. An acid with a base makes a salt and water (HOH). The partners trade, but it is called a neutralization, not a double replacement.
3. Combustion is kept for a compound of carbon and hydrogen reacting with O₂ to make CO₂ and H₂O. A metal burning in O₂ makes one product: a synthesis.

## Graded questions: the pools

1. **A general form** (S, D, SR or DR): which type has it.
2. **A rule** (one per student):
   - HCl + NaOH → NaCl + HOH: the partners trade places. What is this reaction called in this class? **Neutralization.** An acid and a base make a salt and water. The partners do trade, but acid + base gets its own name: neutralization.
   - 2 Mg + O₂ → 2 MgO: magnesium burns with a bright flame. What type of reaction is it? **Synthesis.** There is only one product, so it is a synthesis. Combustion is kept for a compound of carbon and hydrogen making CO₂ and H₂O.
   - C₃H₅(NO₃)₃ → CO₂ + H₂O + N₂ + O₂ (not balanced yet) makes CO₂ and H₂O. What type of reaction is it? **Decomposition.** There is only one reactant, so it is a decomposition. Nothing reacts with O₂ here: oxygen is a product.
   - What does every synthesis reaction have? **One product.** Synthesis: however many reactants, and whether they are elements or compounds, they join into one product.
   - What does every decomposition reaction have? **One reactant.** Decomposition: one reactant breaks into two or more products, which may be elements or compounds.
3. **Which of these is a ... reaction?** Four balanced equations of four different types from the pool above.
4. **What type does this picture show?** A particle picture of a small balanced reaction from the pool.
