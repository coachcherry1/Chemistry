Using the series (single replacement)

  • A metal replaces any metal below it from a compound. If it is not below, there is no reaction.
  • A metal above H2 replaces the hydrogen of an acid; H2 forms.
  • Only the metals from Li to Na replace hydrogen from cold water (HOH); a metal hydroxide and H2 form.

Solubility Rules for Ionic Compounds in Water

Always soluble: compounds of the Group 1 ions (Li+, Na+, K+, Rb+, Cs+) and of ammonium, NH4+.

Using the rules (double replacement)

  • Trade the partners of the two compounds and look up both products.
  • Find the negative ion first, then check whether the positive ion is an exception.
  • An insoluble product is a solid, (s): a reaction happens. If both products are soluble, (aq), there is no reaction.
  • Hg22+ is the mercury(I) ion.
