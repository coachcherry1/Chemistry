# Predicting products: answer key

Generated by `node tools/answer-key.js`. Do not edit by hand.

Each student gets 4 graded questions and 10 graded problems: five single replacements (three metals with a salt, one metal with an acid or with cold water, one halogen; two of the five do not react) and five double replacements (one gives no reaction). Every step of a problem is one graded item, 30 in all, so the score is items right the first time / 34. A single replacement has no states (they do not matter there); a double replacement does.

## The reference on screen

The same data makes the printable sheet, `../reference-sheet/Activity_Series_and_Solubility_Rules.pdf`.

**Activity series, most active first:** Li, Cs, Rb, K, Ba, Sr, Ca, Na, Mg, Al, Mn, Zn, Cr, Fe, Cd, Co, Ni, Sn, Pb, **H₂**, Cu, Ag, Hg, Pt, Au. Halogens: F₂ > Cl₂ > Br₂ > I₂.

- Li to Na: react with cold water, with steam and with acids
- Mg to Cd: react with steam and with acids
- Co to Pb: react with acids
- Cu to Au: do not replace hydrogen

**Solubility.** Always soluble: compounds of group 1 (Li+, Na+, K+, Rb+, Cs+) and of NH₄+. Rows marked * are on the printed sheet only.

## The lesson (not graded)

**Reading the activity series**

1. Zn + CuSO₄ → ZnSO₄ + Cu. Zinc is above copper in the activity series, so it takes its place.
2. Cu + MgCl₂ → no reaction. Copper is below magnesium in the activity series, so it cannot take its place: no reaction.
3. Mg + 2 HCl → MgCl₂ + H₂. Magnesium is above hydrogen in the activity series, so it takes its place.
4. Ag + HCl → no reaction. Silver is below hydrogen in the activity series, so it cannot take its place: no reaction.
5. 2 Na + 2 HOH → 2 NaOH + H₂. Sodium is one of the metals that react with cold water (lithium to sodium on the series), so it takes the place of a hydrogen in HOH.
6. Zn + HOH → no reaction. Zinc does not react with cold water: only the metals from lithium to sodium do. No reaction.
7. Cl₂ + 2 NaBr → 2 NaCl + Br₂. Chlorine is above bromine in the halogens, so it takes its place.
8. I₂ + KCl → no reaction. Iodine is below chlorine in the halogens, so it cannot take its place: no reaction.

**Reading the solubility rules**

1. NaNO₃, sodium nitrate: **(aq)**
2. AgCl, silver chloride: **(s)**
3. BaSO₄, barium sulfate: **(s)**
4. K₂CO₃, potassium carbonate: **(aq)**
5. CaCO₃, calcium carbonate: **(s)**
6. Ca(OH)₂, calcium hydroxide: **(s)**
7. (NH₄)₃PO₄, ammonium phosphate: **(aq)**
8. Ba(OH)₂, barium hydroxide: **(aq)**

**Worked problems**

- Predict the products: a metal and a salt: 2 Al + 3 CuCl₂ → 2 AlCl₃ + 3 Cu. Aluminum is above copper in the activity series, so it takes its place.
- Predict the products: a metal and an acid: Mg + 2 HCl → MgCl₂ + H₂. Magnesium is above hydrogen in the activity series, so it takes its place.
- Predict the products: a solid forms: Pb(NO₃)₂(aq) + 2 KI(aq) → PbI₂(s) + 2 KNO₃(aq). Lead(II) iodide, PbI₂, is insoluble: a solid forms, so there is a reaction.
- Predict the products: or does it?: NaCl(aq) + KNO₃(aq) → no reaction. Both possible products, NaNO₃ and KCl, are soluble. Nothing leaves the solution: no reaction.

## Graded questions

1. Which of four metals will replace aluminum, zinc or iron: the one above it in the series.
2. Which of four metals will not react with hydrochloric acid: copper or silver (below hydrogen).
3. Which of four compounds is insoluble: by the rules.
4. Which of four pairs of solutions gives no reaction: the pair whose two products are both soluble.
