## The lesson (not graded)

**Reading the activity series**

1. Zn(s) + CuSO₄(aq) → ZnSO₄(aq) + Cu(s). Zinc is above copper in the activity series, so it takes its place.
2. Cu(s) + MgCl₂(aq) → no reaction. Copper is below magnesium in the activity series, so it cannot take its place: no reaction.
3. Mg(s) + 2 HCl(aq) → MgCl₂(aq) + H₂(g). Magnesium is above hydrogen in the activity series, so it takes its place.
4. Ag(s) + HCl(aq) → no reaction. Silver is below hydrogen in the activity series, so it cannot take its place: no reaction.
5. Cl₂(aq) + 2 NaBr(aq) → 2 NaCl(aq) + Br₂(l). Chlorine is above bromine in the halogens, so it takes its place.
6. I₂(aq) + KCl(aq) → no reaction. Iodine is below chlorine in the halogens, so it cannot take its place: no reaction.

**Reading the solubility table**

1. NaNO₃, sodium nitrate: **(aq)**
2. AgCl, silver chloride: **(s)**
3. BaSO₄, barium sulfate: **(s)**
4. K₂CO₃, potassium carbonate: **(aq)**
5. CaCO₃, calcium carbonate: **(s)**
6. Cu(OH)₂, copper(II) hydroxide: **(s)**
7. (NH₄)₃PO₄, ammonium phosphate: **(aq)**

**Worked problems**

- Predict the products: a metal and a salt: 2 Al(s) + 3 CuCl₂(aq) → 2 AlCl₃(aq) + 3 Cu(s). Aluminum is above copper in the activity series, so it takes its place.
- Predict the products: a metal and an acid: Mg(s) + 2 HCl(aq) → MgCl₂(aq) + H₂(g). Magnesium is above hydrogen in the activity series, so it takes its place.
- Predict the products: a solid forms: Pb(NO₃)₂(aq) + 2 KI(aq) → PbI₂(s) + 2 KNO₃(aq). Lead(II) iodide, PbI₂, is insoluble: a solid forms, so there is a reaction.
- Predict the products: or does it?: NaCl(aq) + KNO₃(aq) → no reaction. Both possible products, NaNO₃ and KCl, are soluble. Nothing leaves the solution: no reaction.

## Graded questions

1. Which of four metals will replace aluminum, zinc or iron: the one above it in the series.
2. Which of four metals will not react with hydrochloric acid: copper or silver (below hydrogen).
3. Which of four compounds is insoluble: by the table.
4. Which of four pairs of solutions gives no reaction: the pair whose two products are both soluble.

## One student, as an example (seed 1)

1. Which of these metals will replace aluminum from a solution of one of its compounds?
   - Zinc
   - **Magnesium**
   - Copper
   - Lead
2. Which of these metals will NOT react with hydrochloric acid, HCl?
   - Zinc
   - **Silver**
   - Aluminum
   - Magnesium
3. Which of these compounds is insoluble in water, so that it would form as a solid?
   - CaCl₂
   - **AgI**
   - FeSO₄
   - (NH₄)₂SO₄
4. Which pair of solutions gives NO reaction when they are mixed?
   - K₃PO₄ and MgBr₂
   - **NaC₂H₃O₂ and NH₄Cl**
   - BaBr₂ and CuSO₄
   - K₃PO₄ and Al₂(SO₄)₃

1. A piece of aluminum is placed in a solution of zinc chloride, ZnCl₂. **2 Al(s) + 3 ZnCl₂(aq) → 2 AlCl₃(aq) + 3 Zn(s)**
2. Bromine, Br₂, is added to a solution of potassium iodide, KI. **Br₂(aq) + 2 KI(aq) → 2 KBr(aq) + I₂(s)**
3. Solutions of potassium nitrate, KNO₃, and ammonium acetate, NH₄C₂H₃O₂, are mixed. **KNO₃(aq) + NH₄C₂H₃O₂(aq) → no reaction**
4. Solutions of sodium phosphate, Na₃PO₄, and iron(II) chloride, FeCl₂, are mixed. **2 Na₃PO₄(aq) + 3 FeCl₂(aq) → 6 NaCl(aq) + Fe₃(PO₄)₂(s)**
5. A piece of magnesium is placed in a solution of silver nitrate, AgNO₃. **Mg(s) + 2 AgNO₃(aq) → Mg(NO₃)₂(aq) + 2 Ag(s)**
6. Solutions of potassium hydroxide, KOH, and magnesium iodide, MgI₂, are mixed. **2 KOH(aq) + MgI₂(aq) → 2 KI(aq) + Mg(OH)₂(s)**
7. Solutions of sodium sulfate, Na₂SO₄, and lead(II) acetate, Pb(C₂H₃O₂)₂, are mixed. **Na₂SO₄(aq) + Pb(C₂H₃O₂)₂(aq) → 2 NaC₂H₃O₂(aq) + PbSO₄(s)**
8. A piece of copper is dropped into sulfuric acid, H₂SO₄. **Cu(s) + H₂SO₄(aq) → no reaction**
9. Solutions of potassium phosphate, K₃PO₄, and barium chloride, BaCl₂, are mixed. **2 K₃PO₄(aq) + 3 BaCl₂(aq) → 6 KCl(aq) + Ba₃(PO₄)₂(s)**
10. A piece of copper is placed in a solution of iron(II) sulfate, FeSO₄. **Cu(s) + FeSO₄(aq) → no reaction**
