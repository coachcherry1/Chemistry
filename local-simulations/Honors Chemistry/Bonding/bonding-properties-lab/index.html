Big picture: the way atoms bond determines the structure of the substance, and that structure determines its properties. Ionic compounds (NaCl, CaCl2) never conduct as solids — their ions are locked in a rigid lattice — but conduct as liquids and in solution, where the ions are free to move. Covalent/molecular substances (sugar, wax) never conduct in any state, because they contain no charged particles at all. That same ionic lattice, held together by countless strong attractions, is also why ionic compounds melt at far higher temperatures than the covalent/molecular substances tested.
