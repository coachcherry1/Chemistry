Density Lab: Identify the Unknown Metal

You'll be given an unknown metal sample. Drag it onto the balance and record its mass, then use water displacement in a graduated cylinder to find its volume: read the water level before dragging the sample in, then read it again after the water rises. From your own recorded numbers, calculate the metal's density and identify it using the reference table.

After each identification, you'll answer one follow-up question that uses the density you just calculated as a conversion factor.

Correctly identify 2 unknown metals (with every step done correctly) to complete this activity. Your progress is saved if you're interrupted, and a completion grade is submitted to your LMS automatically when you finish.

Before you close your Chromebook or exit Schoology, click the green "Save Progress" button so your work is saved and you can pick up where you left off next time.
