# Reactions and Stoichiometry — Simulation Index

**Course:** AP Chemistry
**Schoology unit:** Reactions and Stoichiometry
**Last updated:** 2026-09-07

Each simulation folder holds `<name>/<name>.html` (or `index.html`) + `imsmanifest.xml`,
with a ready-to-upload `<name>-scorm.zip` beside it. SCORM 1.2, completion-graded,
no student-name gate.

## Simulations

### `naoh-standardization` — NaOH Standardization Lab (v1)
Titrate a NaOH solution against a KHP primary standard to determine its exact molarity.
Kept as-is; superseded for classroom use by v2 below.

### `naoh-standardization-v2` — NaOH and KMnO₄ Standardization Virtual Lab
Two experiments, both required for completion:
- **Experiment 1** — the KHP/NaOH acid–base standardization from v1.
- **Experiment 2** — dilute a KMnO₄ stock in a volumetric flask, then standardize it by
  redox titration against a weighed mass of ferrous ammonium sulfate
  (MnO₄⁻ + 5 Fe²⁺ + 8 H⁺ → Mn²⁺ + 5 Fe³⁺ + 4 H₂O). Includes a redox concept modal
  (oxidation states, what is oxidized/reduced) that gates the titration.
Sized to fit a Chromebook screen.

### `net-ionic-equations-v2` — Net Ionic Equations Practice
For each reaction, write the molecular → complete ionic → net ionic equation, toggling
spectator ions. 10 problems: 3 precipitation double-replacements, 3 strong-acid/strong-base
neutralizations, 2 weak-acid/strong-base (the weak acid stays intact through every step),
and 2 "tricky polyatomic" cases that produce a weak diprotic acid.
