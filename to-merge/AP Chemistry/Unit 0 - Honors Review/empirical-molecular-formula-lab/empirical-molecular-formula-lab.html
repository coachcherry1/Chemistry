Empirical & Molecular Formula Lab Challenge

Work through four laboratory scenarios to determine unknown chemical formulas from experimental data.

Before You Begin

  • You will analyze data from 4 laboratory scenarios: one synthesis reaction, one composition analysis, one hydrate dehydration, and one combustion analysis.
  • Each scenario is broken into small steps. Answer each step using the dropdown menu provided.
  • If a step is incorrect, you'll get a chance to reconsider and try again.
  • Complete all four labs — your score is submitted automatically as your grade for this assignment.
  • A first-attempt score of 70% or higher is required to complete this as a graded assignment. If you score below that, you'll retry the full set.
  • If you get interrupted partway through, your progress is saved — reopening the assignment will pick up right where you left off.
  • Before you close your Chromebook or exit Schoology, click the green "Save Progress" button so your work is saved and you can pick up where you left off next time.
