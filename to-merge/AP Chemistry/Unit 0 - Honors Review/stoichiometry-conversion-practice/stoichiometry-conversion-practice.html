Before You Begin

  • You will solve 5 stoichiometry problems: mass-to-mass, mass-to-volume (STP), mass-to-moles, moles-to-moles, and volume-to-moles.
  • Each problem gives you a balanced chemical equation — read the coefficients carefully, they are not always 1:1.
  • Type your final numeric answer only (no units) using exactly 3 significant figures.
  • If you miss a problem, it will come back later with different numbers — you must answer all 5 correctly to finish.
  • The numbers are randomized, so no two attempts are exactly the same.
  • Once you answer all 5 correctly, a completion grade is automatically submitted to Schoology.
  • If you're interrupted, your progress is saved — reopening the assignment picks up right where you left off.
  • Before you close your Chromebook or exit Schoology, click the green "Save Progress" button so your work is saved and you can pick up where you left off next time.
