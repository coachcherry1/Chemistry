Binary Ionic Compound Builder

You're given the name. Find the elements, work out the ions, then write the formula.

How it works

  1. You'll see the name of a binary ionic compound. Click its cation (metal) on the periodic table first, then click its anion (nonmetal). Any element on the table is fair game, not just the ones this activity happens to use.
  2. Once you've clicked the correct cation and then the correct anion, a window pops up: pick each ion's charge and how many atoms of it the formula needs.
  3. Click Check. If your ion setup is right, a second window lets you build the actual formula. Click the subscript under each symbol to cycle it to the correct number.
  4. Check your formula, review the summary, and move on. Complete all 10 compounds to finish the activity. Your progress is saved if you're interrupted, and a completion grade is submitted to Schoology automatically when you finish.
  5. Before you close your Chromebook or exit Schoology, click the green "Save Progress" button so your work is saved and you can pick up where you left off next time.
