# Math and Measurements — Simulation Index

**Course:** Honors Chemistry
**Schoology unit:** Math and Measurements
**Last updated:** 2026-09-07

Each simulation folder holds `<name>/index.html` (or `<name>.html`) + `imsmanifest.xml`,
with a ready-to-upload `<name>-scorm.zip` beside it. All are SCORM 1.2, completion-graded,
no student-name gate.

## Dimensional analysis (drag-and-drop conversion factors)

A graded series — students build a chain of conversion-factor tiles so every unit cancels.

### `DimAnalysis-0-All-Levels-Student-Picks`
Student chooses which difficulty level to practice; pulls from all of the banks below.

### `DimAnalysis-1-One-Step-Metric`
Single-step metric prefix conversions (mm ↔ m, kg ↔ g, …).

### `DimAnalysis-2-Metric-US-Customary`
One-step conversions that cross between metric and US customary units.

### `DimAnalysis-3-Two-Steps`
Two-factor conversions.

### `DimAnalysis-4-Squared-Cubic`
Area and volume units — conversion factors raised to the 2nd and 3rd power.

### `DimAnalysis-5-Rates`
Compound-unit rate conversions (e.g. km/hr → m/s).

### `DimAnalysis-6-Mixed-Challenge`
Mixed problems drawing on every skill above.

## Significant figures

### `sig-fig-counting` — Significant Figures: Counting Practice
Count the significant figures in a given measurement.

### `sig-fig-calculations-simple` — Significant Figures: Calculations (Simple)
Apply the sig-fig rules to a single operation (add/subtract or multiply/divide).

### `sig-fig-calculations-advanced` — Significant Figures: Calculations (Advanced)
Multi-step calculations where the add/subtract and multiply/divide rules interact.

## Measurement / lab skills

### `measuring-liquids` — Laboratory Measurement Simulation
Read a randomly generated lab instrument (beaker, graduated cylinder, buret) to the
correct precision and number of significant figures.
**Completion:** 10 correct readings (unlimited retries; auto-advances after a correct answer).

### `density-unknown-metal` — Density Lab: Identify the Unknown Metal
Measure mass on a balance and volume by water displacement, calculate density, and match
it to an unknown metal.
