Rules reminder: For + / −, round your answer to match the fewest decimal places of any value used. For × / ÷, round your answer to match the fewest significant figures of any value used. Include a decimal point and trailing zeros when they're needed to show the correct precision (e.g. "5.00" not "5").

Whole numbers in + / − problems: a whole number with no digits past the decimal point (like 500) is only as precise as its last known place, since trailing zeros aren't automatically significant. So "500" is only known to the hundreds place. That means 500 + 5.5 rounds to 500, not 505.5 or 506, because the hundreds place is the least precise place in the problem.

Scientific notation: some answers can't be written precisely as a plain number. For example, 2 sig figs of 29650 rounds to 30000, but written that way it looks like only 1 sig fig (trailing zeros with no decimal point aren't significant, same rule as above). Click the ×10n button to enter your answer as a mantissa and an exponent instead (e.g. mantissa "3.0", exponent "4" for 3.0 × 104).
