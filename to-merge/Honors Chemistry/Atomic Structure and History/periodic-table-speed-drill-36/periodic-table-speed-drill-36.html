How it works

  • You'll see the name of an element. Click that element anywhere on the periodic table below.
  • Only elements 1–36 (highlighted) are used — the rest of the table is shown for reference but is grayed out.
  • Correct answers turn green and build your streak. A wrong answer shows a red ×, highlights the correct element in green, and resets your streak.
  • Any element you miss comes back later in the round — not right away — so you can practice it again.
  • The clock starts the moment you click Start and stops once you've correctly identified all 36 elements.
  • When you finish, show your screen or a screenshot of your results.
  • Your progress is saved if you're interrupted, and a completion grade is submitted to Schoology automatically when you finish.
  • Before you close your Chromebook or exit Schoology, click the green "Save Progress" button so your work is saved and you can pick up where you left off next time.
