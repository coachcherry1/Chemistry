How This Activity Works

Your Task

Each problem shows an unknown element "X" with the mass and percent abundance of 2–3 of its naturally occurring isotopes. Calculate the weighted-average atomic mass, then use that value to identify which real element X is.

Submitting an Answer

  1. Enter your calculated atomic mass, rounded to the hundredths place (2 decimal places).
  2. Click Choose Element and select the matching element from the periodic table.
  3. Click Check Answer to submit both together. Nothing is graded automatically.

Scoring

  • Complete 5 problems correctly to finish the activity.
  • Each incorrect attempt lowers your score by 3%, down to a floor of 60%.
  • You can retry the same problem as many times as you need — there's no attempt limit.

Saving & Submission

Your progress is saved automatically if you're interrupted. Your completion grade and score are submitted to Schoology automatically when you finish.

  • Before you close your Chromebook or exit Schoology, click the green "Save Progress" button so your work is saved and you can pick up where you left off next time.
