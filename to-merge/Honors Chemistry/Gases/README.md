## Simulations

### `gas-collection-over-water` — Gas Collection Over Water
Run two gas-generating reactions (H₂O₂ decomposition and Zn + HCl), read the collected
gas volume off an eudiometer, look up the water-vapor pressure at the given temperature,
and apply Dalton's law of partial pressures to find the dry-gas pressure. Reactant mass
is also entered.
**Completion:** both reactions completed correctly (unlimited retries, no skip).
