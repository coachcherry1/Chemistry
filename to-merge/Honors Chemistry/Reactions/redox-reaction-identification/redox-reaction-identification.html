Oxidation-state rules

  • Free element (Na, O₂, Cl₂): 0
  • Monatomic ion: equals its charge
  • Group 1 metal in a compound: +1
  • Group 2 metal in a compound: +2
  • Oxygen: usually −2 (peroxide −1)
  • Hydrogen: +1 with nonmetals, −1 in metal hydrides
  • Fluorine in a compound: −1
  • Sum of oxidation states = overall charge (0 for a neutral formula)
