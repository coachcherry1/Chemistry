Naming Guidelines

  • Transition metals: Use Roman numerals for the charge — Iron(III) chloride
  • Polyatomic ions: SO₄²⁻ sulfate, NO₃⁻ nitrate, NH₄⁺ ammonium, OH⁻ hydroxide, CO₃²⁻ carbonate
  • -ate vs -ite: -ate has more oxygens, -ite has fewer
  • Covalent: Use prefixes (mono-, di-, tri-, tetra-, penta-...)
