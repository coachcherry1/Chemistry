Coulomb's Law & Attraction

How proton charge and distance control the pull on valence electrons

In every atom, electrons are held in place because opposite charges attract — the positive protons in the nucleus pull on the negative electrons around them. That pull isn't the same strength in every atom, though. In this activity you'll use two small interactive labs and a set of Bohr models to figure out exactly what makes that attraction stronger or weaker, and answer questions about it as you go.

  • Work through each part in order — a question has to be answered correctly before the next part unlocks.
  • If an answer isn't quite right, you'll get feedback to help you think it through again — try as many times as you need.
  • At the end you'll be asked to state the two big rules yourself before the summary appears.
