### Checkpoint 1: chirality (4 of 11)

**★ `a-chiral`** What does it mean for a molecule to be chiral?

- ✓ It cannot be turned to fit exactly onto its mirror image
- It has a mirror plane
- It contains a halogen atom
- It has more than one carbon atom

> Chiral means handed. Like your left and right hands, the molecule and its mirror image cannot be laid on each other exactly, however you turn them. A molecule with a mirror plane is achiral.
